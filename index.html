What is happening right now

The demand

Protesters want the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over alleged irregularities in voter-roll revision (the "special intensive revision", SIR).

10 Oct crackdown

AP reports more than 2,000 protesters detained in Delhi, including opposition lawmakers. Reuters photos and ABC reporting say journalists and lawyers were also held. Most were released that night.

Leaders held at the airport

Founder Abhijeet Dipke and two leaders were detained as their flight landed. A court released them Sunday on a "no breach of peace for one year" condition.

13 Oct, Jantar Mantar

Delhi Police de-listed Jantar Mantar as a protest site. About 20 volunteers, including Ashutosh Ranka and Saurav Das, entered anyway and were detained (Al Jazeera).

Sources: Al Jazeera (10 & 13 Oct 2026), AP/PBS (11 Oct), CNN, ABC News. Only reported facts are stated here. Add a source link to everything you contribute.

Know your rights

Peaceful protest is not a favour. It is written into the Constitution.

Article 19(1)(a)

Freedom of speech and expression

Article 19(1)(b)

The right to assemble peacefully and without arms

Article 21

Protection of life and personal liberty

Article 22

Know the grounds of arrest; consult a lawyer; be produced before a magistrate within 24 hours
Inquilab Zindabad. We the people. Peaceful. Loud. Not going anywhere.

If you are detained

1. Stay calm. Ask: "Am I being arrested or detained? On what grounds?"
2. You can ask to call a lawyer and a family member.
3. Do not sign anything you have not read.
4. Note the officers' names or badge numbers and which police station you are taken to.
5. Tell a trusted person your location before you go to a protest.
